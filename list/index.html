Get your roastery listed

Listing is free. Every new coffee you put in your shop appears in the daily list, with a recipe for each drinker's own brewer and grinder and a link straight to your shop to buy it.

What you get.
  1. Your new coffees in the daily list within a day of going on sale, in front of people who brew at home.
  2. A recipe for every coffee: grind for their grinder, temperature, ratio and every pour, for V60, Chemex, AeroPress, French press and more.
  3. A page per coffee and one for your roastery, so search engines send people looking for your coffee to you.
How it works.

Once a day we read your shop's public product list, as a search engine would, under our own name. We never log in, buy or copy your photos and descriptions: only each coffee's name, origin, process, variety, roast, tasting notes, price and a link to your page. Ask and we stop reading it.

Ask to be listed.

Sending this lets us read your shop's public product list once a day to list your coffees.

Ask to be removed.

Roasters can ask to be removed at any time. Your coffees come off the site within a day, and we don't add them back unless you ask.

The email shows the request comes from the roastery, and we use it only to reply. Nothing else on this site collects or keeps personal data. Privacy.
