Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no ads. What you save stays on your device.

On your device.
  • Your equipment, water, bags, journal and settings are kept in your browser's own storage on this device. They are never sent to us.
  • For you is worked out on this device from the coffees you save.
  • When you scan a bag, the photo is read on the phone and never uploaded.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes all of it.
What other services see.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any host, Cloudflare sees each visit's network address in order to send the page. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: it sets no cookies, doesn't fingerprint or follow you across sites, and gives us only totals (pages viewed, countries, which site sent you, device type), never who you are.
  • The first time you scan a bag, the label reader (Tesseract) is downloaded from jsDelivr, which sees that download.
  • Links to a roaster's shop take you to their site, under their own privacy policy.
Roasters.
  • Once a day we read each roaster's public product list, under our own name and following their robots.txt. We keep each coffee's name, origin, process, variety, roast, tasting notes, price and link, and never copy photos or descriptions.
  • If you ask to be listed or removed, the details you send go into a private record and are used only to reply about your request. Ask and we delete them.
  • Roasters can ask to be removed at any time. Your coffees come off the site within a day.
