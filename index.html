        Taste

        Every bag is different and so is every brew: the roast, your mood, the grind, your tastebuds that day, the water, your nose, the pour. None of it sits still, and that’s the fun of it. A brew log: dose, ratio, grinder settings, kettle temperature and drawdown, all on record. Change one variable per brew, keep what works and repeat it to the gram and the second. Dialled in once, dialled in every time. This is a starting point, not a finish line. Brew it, taste it, write down what you got and the best cup you’ve ever tasted might be just one brew away. Brew something good.

        Disclaimer

        Coffees come from roasters’ own shop listings, checked every day. Not affiliated with any roaster. Roasters can ask to be removed at any time. Your journal, bags, equipment, scan photos and settings never leave this device.

        Methods
        • V60: James Hoffmann’s Better 1-Cup and Ultimate V60, Tetsu Kasuya’s 4:6 and 10-Pour, Scott Rao’s bloom & spin, Onyx Coffee Lab’s V60, CAFEC’s Osmotic Flow, Matt Winton’s Five-Pour, Lance Hedrick’s double bloom.
        • Kalita Wave: George Howell’s 155 guide.
        • April Brewer: April Coffee’s base recipe.
        • Orea: Martin Woelfl’s 2024 World Brewers Cup recipe.
        • Melitta: Revelator Coffee’s Melitta pour over guide.
        • Origami: Carlos Medina’s 2023 World Brewers Cup recipe.
        • Chemex: Blue Bottle’s brew guide.
        • Hario Switch: Tetsu Kasuya’s Hybrid Method.
        • Clever Dripper: James Hoffmann’s Ultimate Clever technique.
        • AeroPress: James Hoffmann’s Ultimate AeroPress technique, standard or inverted; Tim Wendelboe’s café recipe.
        • French Press: James Hoffmann’s Ultimate French Press technique.
        • Espresso: the modern 1:2 in about 25–30 s, after James Hoffmann’s Understanding Espresso series; longer for lighter roasts, shorter for a ristretto.
        • Bialetti Moka Express: James Hoffmann’s Ultimate Moka Pot technique.
        • Moccamaster: Moccamaster’s coffee to water guide, at specialty roasters’ 60 g a litre.
        • Cold brew: Ceremony Coffee’s guide.
        • Grind: Comandante C40 clicks, counted from zero. Other grinders converted from those clicks with Honest Coffee Guide’s grind charts.
